## Points not reviewed by a lawyer

The Steward adopted this agreement without legal review. The following points are
known to be unconfirmed. They do not change the terms above, but contributors should
be aware of them, and a later version may change these sections after review.

- Japanese law: whether a GitHub click-through acceptance (section 11) is sufficient
  evidence of agreement, and whether the section 6 moral-rights non-exercise clause is
  effective (Japanese moral rights cannot be waived or assigned).
- Japanese law: whether a non-exclusive license with sublicensing (section 3) is
  enough for proprietary relicensing without an assignment (Copyright Act arts. 27 and
  28 concern assignment, not licensing).
- Consumer and mandatory law in contributors' countries (EU, US, China): whether the
  choice of Japanese law and Tokyo courts is enforceable against individuals.
- Patent clause (section 5): scope, termination and interaction with AGPLv3 section 11.
- AI disclosure (section 7 item 3): whether the duty is proportionate and how it
  affects the ownership of AI-assisted material.
