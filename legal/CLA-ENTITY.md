## 1. Scope

"Contribution" has the meaning in the Individual agreement, but covers Contributions
submitted by the authorised contributors listed above, and by other people the Entity
later designates in writing, in the course of their work for the Entity.

## 2. Terms

The Entity grants the Steward and recipients the same copyright license (section 3),
patent license (section 5) and moral-rights undertaking (section 6) as the Individual
agreement, for all Contributions in scope, and receives the same promise from the
Steward (section 4). Sections 2, 8, 9, 10 and 12 of the Individual agreement apply
with "you" read as the Entity.

## 3. Entity representations

The Entity represents that:

1. it is legally entitled to grant these licenses for the Contributions in scope,
   including rights it holds as employer or commissioning party;
2. the signatory is authorised to sign on its behalf; and
3. it will update the list of authorised contributors when people join or leave, and
   that the representations in section 7 of the Individual agreement are true for
   Contributions in scope.

Each authorised contributor also accepts the Individual agreement for any rights they
personally hold.

## 4. Signature

The Entity signs by the method published in
[CONTRIBUTOR-LICENSING](../CONTRIBUTOR-LICENSING.md) (a signed PDF or an electronic
signature sent to the Steward). The Steward keeps the signed copy outside the public
repository.
