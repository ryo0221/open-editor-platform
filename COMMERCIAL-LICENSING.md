# Commercial/OEM Licensing — Open Editor Platform

## Two alternative paths
1. **AGPLv3-only:** Commercial redistribution and commercial SaaS are permitted without an OEM fee when the actual combined work and deployment meet AGPLv3 requirements, including corresponding source and network user provisions where applicable.
2. **Separate signed commercial/OEM agreement:** For businesses that require additional rights to create or distribute closed-source covered combinations or use the software outside AGPL conditions. Permissions exist only once all required rights holders have authorized the relevant commercial deal.

## Product-focused, low-overhead business model
The intended commercial offer is a per-product negotiated license, potentially an annual flat fee. The licensor does not undertake dedicated integration, customer support, a response-time SLA, routine bug fixing, security maintenance, or hosting by default. Licensees carry out integration, tests, end-customer support, security remediation, operations and certifications themselves. Any warranty, support or indemnity must be separately written and explicitly priced.

## Terms for an actual commercial agreement
Identify contracting entity and rightsholders, covered source/version, product and distribution topology, territory, duration, renewals, downstream recipients, licensing fees, attribution and third-party rights, modifications, patents, warranty allocation, liability, termination, treatment of deployed copies and audit if relevant. These are **not** supplied by this guide.

Do not imply that closed-source use automatically generates an invoice, or that an unexecuted agreement creates a right to collect a pre-set royalty. An operator can rely on a valid prior MIT release if it lawfully obtained that release.

## Questions about combined programs
A separately authored proprietary Editor extension may be a separate work or may be combined with AGPL platform code depending on the architecture. The additional permission in [ADDITIONAL-PERMISSION-DRAFT](ADDITIONAL-PERMISSION-DRAFT.md) is not active; no blanket exception is promised.

**Publication/contract gate:** Before external commercial licensing, verify chain of title, contributor sublicensing permissions, third-party licenses and any needed patent rights; finalize the B2B form with qualified legal counsel. No fee quote and no support service are offered by this draft notice.
