# Contributor rights — dual AGPL/commercial project

All contributors retain ownership of their original eligible contributions. The project publishes under AGPLv3 and also intends to offer alternative commercial/OEM licenses for authorized code.

Because contributors retain their copyrights, **a PR alone does not grant the project the rights necessary to relicense contributions to proprietary customers**.

## Contributor License Agreement

Before a code PR is merged, the contributor must accept the project's Contributor License Agreement (CLA):

- Individuals: [Individual CLA](legal/CLA-INDIVIDUAL.md) ([日本語参考訳](legal/CLA-INDIVIDUAL.ja.md)).
- Contributors working for a company or other organisation: the organisation also signs the [Entity CLA](legal/CLA-ENTITY.md) ([日本語参考訳](legal/CLA-ENTITY.ja.md)), or confirms in writing that it claims no rights in the contribution.

In short, the CLA lets contributors keep their copyright and grants the project steward a non-exclusive license to distribute contributions under AGPL-3.0-only (with any AGPLv3 section 7 additional permission the steward attaches), under other OSI-approved licenses, and under separate commercial licenses, plus a patent license limited to the contribution. In return the steward promises to keep every version that contains the contribution available under an OSI-approved license.

**Status: version 1.0 adopted on 2026-11-14.** It has not been reviewed by a lawyer; each agreement lists the points that are unconfirmed.

## How to accept

**Individuals:** post the following comment on your pull request, from the GitHub account that made the commits:

> I have read the Open Editor Platform Individual CLA version 1.0 and I agree to it.

One acceptance covers all your later pull requests until you end it in writing. The maintainer records your GitHub account, the pull request, the CLA version and the date outside the public repository. A CLA bot may later post this request and keep the record automatically; the comment above stays valid.

**Entities:** send a signed PDF or an electronic signature of the Entity CLA to the steward through a channel named in the pull request discussion. The signed copy is kept outside the public repository.

**Exception:** trivial documentation fixes (such as typos or broken links) may be merged without a CLA at the maintainer's discretion.

## Other checks

Contributor employers, third-party code, generative-AI provenance, license notice obligations and compatibility must be checked. The maintainer may reject a PR for technical, security, licensing or maintenance reasons. **Users do not have to submit a PR to use their own extension privately or under any otherwise available lawful permissions.**

Any independent Editor exception must be expressly granted by the copyright holders of the code to which it applies; it is not silently created by this contributor policy. The CLA allows the steward to attach such a permission to contributed code, but no such exception is in effect until it is adopted.
