# bim-core (R2c-2, pure building model)

Private pure TypeScript model for the building (BIM) domain of the R2c design. Not a registered editor, a stable public API or an accepted storage format. No DOM, React, Worker or geometry kernel, and no import of any CAD package: `bim` is a separate domain that shares only the geometry boundary (`geometry-core`, ADR 017).

- `model.ts`: `schemaVersion: 1` types, the only schema (`BimDocument`: project, levels, grids, materials, types, elements, joins, views, property sets), millimetres, right-handed Z-up. Points are plan coordinates or grid intersections; heights are a level plus an offset, or a fixed height. Types: layered wall/slab/roof, column/beam profiles, door/window sizes, part references (`{ artifactId, versionId, contentHash }` plus hint bounds). Elements: wall (straight or arc by bulge), slab with holes, column, beam, opening in a wall or slab, door, window, roof (flat or mono-pitch), space, part. `bimLimits` and `bimTolerance` hold the initial safety limits.
- `validateBim(unknown)`: strict plain-JSON validation with path diagnostics: exact keys, one ID namespace, reference kinds, storey and section limits, loop simplicity, property values against their sets (reserved `EP_`, `Pset_`, `Qto_` names refused), then the derived-geometry diagnostics. Any `schemaVersion` other than 1 is `unsupported-schema`; there is no migration.
- `deriveBim(doc)`: pure derived shapes and quantities. Wall footprints follow stored joins (`butt` with a through wall, `miter`) for line–line L and T joins; a join that does not fit is refused by name (`join-not-touching`, `join-height-mismatch`, `join-thickness-mismatch`, `join-through`, `join-unsupported`, `join-failed`). Openings are refused outside their host, overlapping each other or in a joined stretch. Quantities (lengths, areas, volumes) are analytic and exact along arcs; a wall or column clipped by a roof (`clipBy`) reports its quantities as `kernel`, left to the kernel adapter.
- `applyBimOperation`: pure, atomic operations (`insert`, `update` keeping the kind, `delete` refusing `in-use` unless cascading, `setProject`), each result validated as a whole. `bimDependents` and `entityReferences` give the dependency closure; `proposeJoins` lists the joins a drawing command would add. The caller's trusted layer assigns every new ID.
- `bimEditorCommand`: one `EditorSession` transaction per operation. It re-checks the expected base `contentHash` (`bim-base-mismatch`) and edit authority (`permission-denied`), and pins `reads.content = ['*']`.
- `ops.ts` (R2c-3a): `bimGeometry(doc)` turns the derived shapes into one `geometry-core` op graph: a wall's footprint prism, clipped by its roof's undersides (`halfSpace`) and cut by its openings; a slab with its holes and openings; a roof's planes each as a prism between its top and underside; a space's prism. Columns, beams, door and window panels and parts are a solid at the origin plus a `placement`, so equal recipes share one solid by their `geometryNodeKey`. A clipped wall also names its solid before openings (`gross`) for its gross volume. Node IDs are the element's ID, a NUL (no element ID holds one) and the node's role (`bimNodeRole`), so elements' nodes never meet.
- `solids.ts`: where solids lie, shared with the IFC export so both describe the same solid: `bimWallAxis`, `bimWallOpeningLoop`, `bimRoofPlanes`, and `bimProfileLoops` (a profile about its bounding box's centre, as IFC4's parameterized profiles).
- `drawing.ts` (R2c-5a): plans and sections, the pure half. `bimViewFrame(doc, view)` is where a view cuts and looks (a plan cuts at its level plus `cutHeightMm` and sees down to `depthMm` below; a section cuts on its line and sees `depthMm` towards `left` or `right`), with `toView` matching the kernel's projection frame. `bimDrawingSymbols` draws by rule, never from the 3D shapes (R2c-U7): door leaves and swings and window marks in the walls the plan cuts, type marks, space numbers, names and areas on the plan's level, grid lines with bubbles and the spacing of neighbouring parallel axes, and in a section the level marks and the axes its line crosses. `bimCutLoops` joins a kernel cut's edges into loops; `bimDrawingSvg` writes a drawing as SVG in model millimetres with line weights and text from the scale (1:50, 1:100, 1:200), the same bytes for the same drawing, and lists elements that were not drawn. Limits: 200,000 primitives, 32 MiB of SVG.
- `fixtures.ts`: `emptyBim`, F31 (`f31BoxBuilding`), F33 (`f33Types`) and F37 (`f37Office`).

Not yet here: line–arc joins, the display and Surface and Host (R2c-3b, R2c-3c). Kernel evaluation is in `packages/bim`; IFC in `packages/bim-ifc`. See the verification record for tested scope.
