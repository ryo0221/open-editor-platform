# geometry-cfd (domain-independent flow, VS2)

Private package of the vehicle simulation plan (VS2, VS-U2). Not a registered editor or a stable public API. It depends on nothing, and never on a CAD, BIM, character or simulation package (the ADR 017 rule for `geometry-*`, enforced by ESLint).

- `voxelize.ts`: `TriangleSurface` (points in mm, triangles, optional CAD face index per triangle) and `Grid` (origin, cubic cell size, cell counts). `checkSurface` refuses an empty surface, bad indices, non-finite points, a missing face index (`surface-invalid`) and any edge not shared by exactly two triangles (`surface-open`). `voxelize(surface, grid)` marks the cells whose centre is inside (ray parity along x, rays nudged off grid lines) and, for each fluid cell next to a solid one, the fraction q ∈ (0, 1] of each link up to the surface (segment–triangle tests on binned triangles; a link that finds no triangle uses ½ and is counted). It refuses grids over `cfdLimits.maxCells` (2,000,000; `grid-too-large`) and bodies without one free cell to every side of the grid (`body-outside`). `frontalArea` is the body's area seen along x.
- `lattice.ts`: the D3Q27 velocities, weights, opposites and the second-order equilibrium.
- `d3q27-kernel.generated.ts`: the collision of one cell, unrolled; written by `scripts/cfd/generate-d3q27-kernel.mjs` (do not edit).
- `lbm.ts`: `Flow`, a wind tunnel on the lattice. The flow enters at x = 0 at speed U (the inlet plane is held at equilibrium) and leaves at the far end (populations carried out unchanged); sides and top are free-slip; the ground is free-slip, fixed or moving at U (a rolling road; half-way bounce-back with the wall speed). Collision is regularised BGK (the non-equilibrium part projected on its second moment) with a Smagorinsky eddy viscosity (Hou et al.); the body uses Bouzidi's linearly interpolated bounce-back, and the force on it is the momentum exchanged across its links (`state.force`, `averageForce` since `resetAverage`). Settings out of range are refused (`config`: 0 < U ≤ 0.15, 0 < ν < 1, 0 ≤ C ≤ 0.3); a speed over 0.45 or a non-finite value stops the run (`unstable`). `fields()` gives density and velocity, `mass()` the fluid's mass. `forceCoefficient(F, U, A)` is F / (½ U² A) with A in cells². All values are lattice units (spacing, step and density 1).
- `fields.ts`: `sampleVelocity` (trilinear between cell centres), `streamline` (midpoint steps until the line leaves the grid, enters the body or stalls) and `surfacePressure` (Cp on each triangle from the first fluid cell along its normal, and the area-weighted mean per CAD face).
- `fixtures.ts`: `icosphere` and `boxSurface`, closed surfaces for tests.

The browser flow is a reference display (VS-U2): a real car's Reynolds number (about 5 × 10⁶) cannot be resolved on these grids, so drag numbers are judged on the Host (OpenFOAM, VS3).
