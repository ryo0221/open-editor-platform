# cad-core (CAD-T1, D1a pure model)

Private pure TypeScript model for the 3D CAD Editor design. Not a registered editor, a stable public API or an accepted storage format. No DOM, React, Worker or geometry kernel: solid validity, meshes and measurements belong to the kernel adapter (CAD-T2) and are not checked here.

- `model.ts`: schema 2 types, the only schema (`CadDocument`, typed `Length`/`Angle`/`Position3` with expression variants, length/angle/count parameters with a value or an expression, unit quaternions, dedicated body placement) and the initial safety limits from model.md and r1-modeling.md. CAD is unreleased: schema 1 and unknown schemas are refused with `unsupported-schema`, never read as schema 2. R2a-1 (r2a-freeform.md): each body has a `kind`, `solid` or `sheet` (a surface without volume); `extrudeSurface` and `revolveSurface` make a sheet from a sketch's open chain or loop (`CadSketchPath`), `featureKind` derives a feature's kind, and validation refuses a body whose kind is not its result's (`body-kind`) and a sheet given where a solid is needed (`sheet-not-solid`). R2a-2b: `surfaceLoft` (a sheet through 2–32 chains of distinct sketches, all open or all loops; `loft-section-closure` otherwise) and `surfaceSweep` (a chain, open or a loop, along an open chain of another sketch) are sheets too. A pick table edge may carry `boundary`, the name a sheet's feature gives a free edge, and `elementName` names such an edge by its face and that boundary instead of its face twice. F17 fixtures (`f17Sheets`, `f17Surfaces`, `f17LoftReference`). R2a-2c: `boundarySurface` (3 or 4 curves) and `fill` (3–16 curves, each `G0` or `G1`) span curves (`CadCurveSource`: a sketch line, arc or open spline, or a referenced edge of a result) in loop order, and a `surfaceLoft` section may be a chain of referenced edges (`CadEdgeChain`). `featureReferenceSources` lists the features those references read, so they order, delete and duplicate with them; validation refuses curve counts, repeated curves, curves without two ends and `G1` on a sketch curve (`fill-no-adjacent-face`). R2a-3: `trim` cuts one sheet with a tool (`CadTrimTool`: a sketch's closed chain projected along its normal, a plane, or another sheet's result, read and not consumed) and keeps a side stored by structure (`inside`/`outside` for a chain, `front`/`back` for a plane or sheet); `sew` joins 2–64 sheets within 0.000001–0.0001 mm, a sheet or, with `solid`, a solid; `thicken` makes a solid of a sheet (`front`, `back` or `both`). The `sewBodies` operation keeps the target body and consumes the others. `cutBoundaryName` and `thickenSideName` name cut edges and side faces by their source, and `remapTopoName` carries `sew:`, `trim:` and `cut:` names through a duplicate. Their boundary edges are named by source (`sketch:<ID>/boundary:e:<element>`, `from:<feature ID>/boundary:(<edge name>)`), which `duplicateClosure` renames. F17 fixtures `f17Boundaries` and `f17HyparArea`. R2b-1 (r2b-assembly.md): `assembly.ts` adds part definitions that name a saved version by content hash (`parts`), fixed components (`components`), the solved poses re-solved by every operation (`solvedPoses`, `settleAssembly`), part properties, and the expansion of nested assemblies (`expandAssembly`) within `cadAssemblyLimits`; `assembly-validation.ts` checks them (joints and component patterns must be empty), `assembly-operations.ts` has `insertComponents`, `setComponentPlacement` and `setPartProperties`, and `CadAssemblyPort` (`assembly-port.ts`) evaluates the document, then each reached version once by hash, with its state (available, substitute, unavailable, failed). R2b-2a: `joints.ts` adds connectors (a face or edge of an active body's result, or the part's origin, on a component path, with flip and offset) and joints (fastened, revolute, slider, cylindrical, planar) with values and limits, and solves the joint tree from given values (`solveJoints`, no constraint solver); `joints-validation.ts` checks the tree (`joint-cycle`, `joint-multiple-parents`, `joint-child-placed`, `component-unplaced`), values and limits (`joint-limit`); `joints-operations.ts` has `insertJoint`, `updateJoint`, `setJointValues`, `solveAssemblyPoses`, and deleting a joint fixes its child where it stands. `CadAssemblyPort` asks the kernel for the connector frames the joints read (this document's on its own evaluation, a part's on its cached one, evaluated again only for a new reference), solves the joints and reports each joint's solution, every component's solved pose and the joints whose stored child pose does not match (`poseMismatch`). The coordinator writes the solved poses into the same edit (`withSolvedPoses`, evaluated again so the ticket reproduces it), refuses an edit that breaks a connector (`connector-ambiguous`, `connector-missing`, `connector-unsupported-geometry`), and holds every edit of a mismatched document but `solveAssemblyPoses` (`assembly-pose-mismatch`). `joints-fixtures.ts` is F20. R2b-2b: `replacePartSources` gives parts other saved versions of their Artifacts in one edit (two parts that come to name one version become one); the port resolves every connector again on the new versions (a sub-assembly's relative path too), and the coordinator refuses a candidate with broken connectors as a `geometry-error` naming every broken joint (`brokenJoints`) and the first one's references with candidates, so `rebindReferences` (now also on a joint's `['parent', 'ref']` and `['child', 'connector', 'ref']`) can choose them again in the same edit. `versions-fixtures.ts` is F21. R2b-2c: a topology reference may name a vertex (`kind: 'vertex'`), named by the faces of its edges (three at a box's corner) and resolved, selected and hinted like faces and edges; only connectors accept one, features refuse it (`reference-kind`). Pick tables carry `vertices` (the kernel's points). R2b-3a: `bom.ts` derives the bill of materials, never stored (`bomRows`: flat, each version once with nested quantities multiplied and versions that only place parts left out; hierarchical, each assembly's own components by version under it, `1.1`…), with this document's active bodies as parts and `bom: 'exclude'` components (and what they place) left out; a version it cannot read shows its stored name only. `bomCsv` writes RFC 4180 CSV and prefixes `'` to cells starting with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, a tab or CR. `setComponentBom` includes or excludes a component. `bom-fixtures.ts` is F22. R2b-3b: `componentPatterns` lay out copies of a direct component in a row or a circle (R1's `CadPatternLayout`; `count` includes the seed). Copy `i` is the path step `copy:<pattern ID>:<i>` (`copyStep`, `parseCopyStep`), never stored: its pose is the pattern's move applied to the seed's (`patternMove`, `stepPose`), `expandAssembly` lists copies right after their seed (nested ones too, within the instance limit) and the BOM counts them with the seed. Validation checks the seed, the layout and at most 4,096 copies per document (`limit-pattern-copies`), and reserves `copy:` component IDs. A joint's parent may be a copy (never its child; a copy hanging its own seed is `joint-cycle`); a count that removes such a copy is refused (`connector-copy-missing`). `insertComponentPattern` and `updateComponentPattern` (same seed) edit them; deleting a seed or a parameter a pattern reads takes the pattern and the joints on its copies along. R2b-4a: with `background`, `CadAssemblyPort` returns once this document and the versions its joints read (every part on a connector's path, sub-assemblies with joints, parts of another build) are evaluated, lists the rest as `pending`, and evaluates them after the result one at a time in `order` (the Surface's largest on screen first); a newer request or a cancel stops it between parts (a running job finishes and is kept, one version is never evaluated twice at once), `resume` goes on with a result's pending versions, `version` gives each one's result and `idle` waits for the run. `scale-fixtures.ts` is F23 (300 brackets placed 1,000 times, nested three deep).
- `continuity.ts`: R2a-4's fixed continuity check of an edge between two faces (`cadContinuity`: 65 samples, G0 0.0001 mm, G1 0.1°, G2 1% of the larger normal curvature, both radii over 10,000 mm flat) and `continuityReport`, which turns the kernel's samples into each level's largest deviation, where it is and whether every sample is within tolerance (a value equal to the tolerance passes). A judgement at the samples only.
- `curvature.ts`: R2a-4's curvature display (`cadCurvature`): `splineComb`, a sketch spline's curvature comb from its exact first and second derivatives (signed by the turn); `combTip` and `combScale` (the longest tooth a fifth of the curve's size); `curvatureRange` (the 95th percentile of the node values' magnitudes) and `curvatureColor`, the diverging map of the curvature shading. Display only.
- `topology.ts`: persistent topology names and references (R1b): face names as segment lists, edges named by their two faces, the evaluation's pick table (`CadTopologyTable`), `resolveTopologyRef` (resolved, ambiguous with candidates nearest the hint first, or missing; never the nearest element by geometry), `selectFor` (the fewest neighbour names that pick one of several elements of one name, or `reference-not-selectable`), `topologyRefOf` (the reference a pick makes: name, `select` and hint) and `featureReferences` (a feature's references with their paths in its args). R1c stores `CadTopologyRef`s in features (fillet and chamfer edges, a chamfer's face, a shell's open faces, a draft's faces and neutral face, a mirror's face): validation checks their shape, that they name an input of their feature and the limits (256 per feature, 4,096 per document); whether they resolve is decided by evaluation, which reports each broken one as a `CadReferenceProblem` with candidates. Names carry IDs (`tool:<feature ID>`, polyline sides `side:v:<vertex ID>`, pattern copies `copy:<pattern ID>:<i>`, `mirror:<mirror ID>`, and the names inside `blend:` and `shared:` segments, a face several Boolean tools share), so `duplicateClosure` renames them in copied references.
- `sketch.ts`: R1d constrained sketches (r1-modeling.md, 拘束付きsketch（F2）): points, lines, arcs and circles (construction allowed) with geometric and dimensional constraints, and `solved`, the stored coordinates evaluation reads (`[u, v]` per point, `[radius, 0]` per circle). Element and constraint IDs are local to their sketch. `solveSketch` is a deterministic Levenberg–Marquardt solve from the stored coordinates (at most 100 steps per connected component) that reports the degrees of freedom left and refuses a redundant or conflicting constraint by name (row rank by Gram–Schmidt); `sketchMismatches` checks stored coordinates against the constraints at 1e-6 mm and 1e-9 rad without solving. `sketchRegion` checks a region (one outer loop and 0–64 holes of element IDs, chained by shared point IDs): closed, no crossing or shared stretch, holes inside the outer loop and outside each other, winding exact around arcs. Operations solve only when an edit changes a sketch or a parameter it reads. For the kernel (R1d-2): `loopArea` (signed, counter-clockwise positive, exact along arcs) and `regionArea`; `attachFrame` places a sketch on a planar face from the face's plane alone (outward normal, origin the world origin projected onto it plus `offset`, `v` the world Z projected onto it, or `u` the world X on a face normal to Z); `sketchReferences` lists a sketch's stored attachment, which `rebindReferences` can choose again (`featureId` is then the sketch's ID and the path `['plane', 'face']`). For the Surface (R1d-3, R1e): `sketchDefaultRegion` (the largest closed loop of a drawing with the loops inside it as holes) and `sketchDefaultPath` (the open chain its lines and arcs make). R1e loft and sweep features read sketches: a loft through 2–32 hole-free regions of distinct sketches (`ruled` or smooth), a sweep of one hole-free region along an open chain of another sketch (`sketchPath` checks it; `frenet` or `fixed` orientation). `featureSketches` lists every sketch a feature reads for the dependency graph. F15 fixtures (`f15Loft`, `f15Sweep`). Splines (R2a-2, r2a-freeform.md): a `spline` element is a non-rational B-spline whose control points (`poles`, 4–256, at least degree + 1) are sketch points, of degree 2, 3 or 5, open (uniform clamped knots, through its end points) or `closed` (periodic); constraints act on its control points, so it adds no degrees of freedom. `splineKnots` gives the knots the kernel builds from and `splinePoints` samples the curve (de Boor); crossings, overlaps, areas and extremes use that sampled polygon (`sketchElementPoints`). Validation refuses a spline that crosses itself (`sketch-self-intersection`), too few or too many control points (`sketch-spline-poles`), another degree (`sketch-spline-degree`) and control points on top of each other (`sketch-degenerate`). An open spline chains like a line or arc in regions and paths; a closed one is a loop alone, like a circle. F16 fixtures (`f16Sketch`, `f16Spline`, `f16Reference`).
- `expression.ts`: schema 2 expressions stored as a syntax tree in postfix order (parameters by ID), their structural check, type rules (length, angle, scalar; a count reads as a scalar), pure evaluation, and the text form for the Inspector (`parseExpression`, `formatExpression`). `evaluateParameters` in `graph.ts` evaluates parameters in dependency order and reports cycles and chains over the limit.
- `validateCad(unknown)`: strict plain-JSON and domain validation with path diagnostics: exact keys, ID uniqueness, parameter types, affine range, quaternion norm, profile simplicity/area, sketch structure, stored solutions and regions, revolve axis side, feature arity/order/cycle, one feature–sketch dependency graph (`cad-dependency-cycle` names the nodes) and its depth, body/consumed/view invariants, limits. D1 rejects `imported` features and non-empty `assets`.
- `applyCadOperation`: pure, atomic operations (`insertFeatures` (with sketches, solved on insert), `setParameter` with a literal or an expression (sketches that read it are solved again), `updateSketch`, `updateFeature`, `updateProfile`, `transformBody`, `booleanBodies` (one tool, or 1–256 `toolIds` in one Boolean), `setBooleanTools` (a Boolean's tools in another order or with some taken out; a tool taken out is an active body again), `patternBodies` (copies of a tool body along a row or around an axis, Booleaned into a target at once; the tool is consumed), `detailBody` (a fillet, chamfer, shell, draft or mirror on a body's result), `rebindReferences` (references chosen again, with the upstream edit that broke them, as one edit), `deleteClosure`, `duplicateClosure`, `rename`, `saveNamedView`, `reorderFeatures`). The caller's trusted layer assigns every new ID before the operation is built.
- `cadEditorCommand`: one ordinary `EditorSession` transaction per operation. It re-checks the expected base `contentHash`, current edit authority and the result schema, and pins `reads.content = ['*']`. The kernel coordinator's prepare/ticket boundary (operations.md) is not in this package; geometry-producing operations must not reach a session without it.
- `compareCad`: semantic differences by stable ID. `fixtures.ts`: F01/F02/F03/F05 from the verification plan and F10–F14 from the R1 design, with F11 (`f11Sketch`, `f11Bracket`).

`fixtureKernel` is a placeholder evaluator identity. The real `kernelProfile.buildHash` is pinned by the D0 kernel gate (CAD-T0). See verification record for tested scope.
