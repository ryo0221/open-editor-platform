# construction-core (4D schedule of a building, its site, cranes, frame, traffic and IFC, CS1–CS6)

Private package of the construction-site plan (CS1, CS-U1). Not a registered editor or a stable public API. Pure model: no React, no kernel, no Worker. It reads BIM only through `@editor-platform/bim-core`'s public entrypoint (the document type and fixtures), and takes only the frame model's types from `@editor-platform/geometry-fem`. The IFC schedule (CS6a) uses `@editor-platform/bim-ifc`'s string and GlobalId helpers and `@editor-platform/geometry-core`'s P21 reader.

- `model.ts`: `ConstructionDocument` schema 1, a separate artifact that fixes one BIM version (`source`: artifact, version, content hash). A start date, a calendar (worked weekdays and holidays) and tasks: name, working days (0 = milestone), finish-to-start predecessors with a lag in working days, an action (`construct`, `remove`, `temporary`) and the BIM element IDs it acts on, with optional actual dates and percent typed in by hand (CS-U7). `constructionLimits` holds the initial safety limits (2,000 tasks, 1,000 elements a task, dates 1900–2200).
- `validation.ts`: `validateConstruction(value)` checks plain JSON, schema 1 only, every key and value, one task ID namespace (`duplicate-id`, `retired-id-in-use`), links (`self-link`, `task-missing`, `duplicate-link`, `cycle` naming the loop), an element held by two tasks of one action or by temporary works and another task (`element-twice`), and actual dates (`actual-order`).
- `schedule.ts`: `scheduleConstruction(doc)` plans by the critical path method in working days (early and late start and finish, total float, critical), refusing a plan past 2200 (`schedule-too-long`) and warning when an element is removed before it is built (`remove-before-construct`). `siteOnDay(doc, schedule, day)` gives each held element's state (`absent`, `building`, `built`, `removing`, `temporary`; elements no task holds are `existing`) and the tasks running; actual dates override the plan.
- `operations.ts`: `applyConstructionOperation` (rename, BIM version, start date, calendar, add, edit, remove a task, replace every task) on a copy, then the whole document is validated; `constructionEditorCommand`, one ordinary `EditorSession` command that re-checks the base hash (`construction-base-mismatch`) and edit authority (`permission-denied`). A task another follows is not removed (`task-in-use`); removed IDs are retired.
- `source.ts`: `checkConstructionSource(doc, bim)` lists task elements missing from the BIM version (`element-missing`); `unscheduledElements` lists the ones no task holds.
- `csv.ts`: `readConstructionCsv` (header `id,name,duration,predecessors,action,elements,actual_start,actual_finish,percent`, lists split by `;`, a lag in brackets `t2(3)`), refused as a whole with problems by line; `writeConstructionCsv` writes the same form.
- `site.ts` (CS2): `ConstructionSite`, the document's optional `site`: a boundary polygon, surveyed ground points (a Delaunay TIN over up to 10,000 points via `delaunator`; flat at 0 without any), earthworks (`cut` or `fill` to a level inside an outline, side slopes as run per rise, done evenly over a task's days) and temporary works (scaffolding, hoarding, gate, yard, site office: an outline and a height, standing from one task's start to another's finish). `validateSite` (called by `validateConstruction`), `siteTerrain`, `earthworkVolumes` (cut and fill in m³, one low-discrepancy sample a cell on a grid of 200 to 2,000 cells a side over the ground near the earthwork, finer for narrow outlines, and a grid of up to 600 a side over ground it reaches farther off), `siteWorksOnDay` (progress, m³ moved and works standing on a day), `groundOnDay`, `checkSite` (`outside-boundary`, `removed-before-put-up`), `checkBuildingInSite` (building elements whose evaluated points lie over the boundary; CS2b), `triangulatePolygon` (ear clipping, for the view) and `readTerrainCsv` (`x,y,z` in mm). Operations `setSiteGround`, `putEarthwork`, `removeEarthwork`, `putTemporary`, `removeTemporary`; a task the site names is not removed.
- `crane.ts` (CS3): cranes and lifts in the site's optional `cranes` and `lifts`. A crane is parametric (CS-U4): a tower crane (mast, a level jib that slews, a trolley) or a mobile crane (a boom from a pivot), with a base, height, jib or boom length, smallest radius and a load chart (radii ascending, capacities not rising; between rows the next row out applies), standing from one task's start to another's finish. A lift carries one element of a `construct` task from a pick point to where the element stands. `validateCranes` (called by `validateSite`), `liftTimes` (a task's lifts spread over its working days, in order), `liftMotion` (hoist to the highest thing standing plus 1 m, slew the short way while the radius changes, lower), `checkCranes(doc, schedule, world)` (`out-of-reach`, `overload`, `mass-unknown`, `hook-height`, `load-clash`, `jib-clash`, `crane-not-standing`, `element-missing`, `slew-overlap`), the world giving each element's bounds and triangles, its mass and the ground. Operations `putCrane`, `removeCrane` (refused while a lift uses it: `crane-in-use`), `putLift`, `removeLift`.
- `frame.ts` (CS4): the frame of a construction stage for the Host. `frameOnDay(doc, schedule, world, day)` takes the columns, beams and members standing that day (built, existing, being removed, or being built and already set by a lift) with a Young's modulus, as lines along their axes (beams at their top level, the floor's; an end on another's span cuts it there; four elements a piece), sections from `frameSection` (exact for rectangle, circle, pipe, box and H; a channel or angle marked approximate), fixed at the column feet on the foundation level (the lowest column foot); pieces reaching no support are left out (`frame-unsupported`). Loads: the members' own weight lumped to element ends; each slab standing or being cast carries its weight, and the site loads (`site.loads`: kN/m² on slabs while a task runs) theirs, shared to the nearest frame node at the slab's level by sample points over the slab (tributary areas; the slab adds no stiffness). `frameCompleted` is the finished building without site loads. `frameMemberResults` and `compareFrames` (the day's stress over the completed one, worst first) read a Host result; `craneReactions` gives each crane foundation the crane's own mass (`massKg`) plus the heaviest load and the largest load × radius. Operations `putSiteLoad`, `removeSiteLoad`.
- `fixtures.ts`: `f85OfficeSchedule()` (F85), the F37 five-storey office built storey by storey in 28 tasks (2027-04-01 to 2027-08-27); `f86OfficeSite()` (F86), F85 on a 75 m × 60 m sloping plot with its pit, a filled yard and five temporary works; `f87Building()` (F87) the F37 office with material densities and `f87OfficeCranes()` (F87) F86 with a 50 m tower crane lifting every column and beam from the yard.; `f88Building()` (F88) adds concrete 24,000 MPa and steel 205,000 MPa, and `f88OfficeFrame()` (F88) F87 with the crane's 60 t and 6 kN/m² of shoring and rebar on the 3F slab while 4F is cast; `f89OfficeTraffic()` (F89) F88 with steel trucks, workers walking to the office and a rebar cutting area while the 1F columns go up.
- `traffic.ts` (CS5a): routes for workers and trucks (a ground polyline, a speed, departures on the working days of a task, out and back), danger zones drawn by hand, the working hours; the trips of a day, the dangers in force (zones, under each lift's load, the crane's slewing circle while it lifts, the top edge of an open cut), every approach within the margin (worker 1 m, truck 2 m) by time, and the trips through each gate.
- `ifc.ts` (CS6a): `writeConstructionIfc(doc, schedule, buildingIfc, options)` adds the schedule to the building's IFC4 file (`IfcWorkSchedule`, `IfcTask` with `IfcTaskTime`, `IfcRelSequence` with `IfcLagTime`, `IfcRelAssignsToControl`, `IfcRelAssignsToProduct` per element) with stable GlobalIds, naming elements the file does not carry; `readConstructionIfc(text)` reads back only files written here (CS-U2).
  CS6b adds each temporary work as an `IfcBuildingElementProxy` (its footprint extruded to its height, in the site, assigned to the tasks it stands from and until).
- `checks.ts` (CS6b): the day's checks as rows (`diagnosticRows`, `approachRows`) and `writeChecksCsv(rows)` (CRLF, values that a spreadsheet would read as a formula prefixed with `'`).
