# cad (CAD-T3)

Private CAD Surface for the 3D CAD design. Not wired into a Host yet (CAD-T4).

- `CadController`: React-free state for the Surface. Owns the `CadCoordinator`, selection, diagnostics (Japanese messages) and meshes keyed by exact content hash. Every edit is prepare → apply; nothing writes the session directly. IDs are assigned once, before prepare.
- `CadSurface`: the Navigator's Surface… dialog makes a sheet from a sketch's chain (extruded, or revolved about the sketch's vertical axis); a sheet's measurements show area and open edges, not volume. `EditorLayout` with tools (Undo, Redo, Box, Cylinder, Sphere, Plate, Move bodies, Save view, Export PNG, Export PNG without background), an element list (bodies, parameters, feature history), an Inspector (name, placement, dimensions, measurements, Combine, Delete) and a status line. A rejected edit restores the field. Documents are schema 2 (R1a): parameter values, literal dimensions and the revolve angle take a number or an expression over parameter names (`expression-input.ts` reads the committed text; a parse error stays in the field with its position), and the Parameters list adds length, angle and count parameters. R1c entry points (R1 modeling, Surface): a picked edge offers Fillet and Chamfer with one size; a picked face offers Shell (that face open), Draft (the faces around a planar face, pulled into the body) and Mirror and join (in a planar face); a body offers Pattern (copies of another body in a row along X, Y or Z or in a circle about Z, cut or joined). Each is one edit that selects the new feature, whose Inspector edits its radius, distance, thickness, angle, count or spacing. A Boolean's Inspector lists its tools in order: each moves up or down, or is taken out and becomes a body again (`setBooleanTools`). A feature with stored references lists them with their state on the current evaluation (Resolved, Ambiguous with its candidates, Missing, or Not checked while recalculating or when the shape failed elsewhere); an entry of an edge or face list can be taken out. When an edit breaks references (ambiguous or missing), it is not refused outright: a panel over the view (the status line points to it, as the narrow Inspector covers the view) shows each broken reference's candidates in turn, each evaluated with the edit as a preview shown undimmed and marked at the candidate; Confirm applies the edit and the choice as one `rebindReferences` edit, Cancel keeps everything. A choice that breaks a later feature continues with that feature's references; a reference with no candidate blocks Confirm. Content that fails on broken references offers the same choice from the failure note. Sketch mode (R1d-3): the Sketches section opens a new sketch on the XY, XZ or YZ plane or on a picked planar face, or edits an existing one. The drawing is a 2D canvas over the view: lines (one stroke, closed on its first point), arcs (centre, start, end), circles and splines (R2a-2: a click per control point, Enter to end it open, a click on its first point to close it; drawn with its control polygon), with clicks near a point sharing it; the select tool picks elements and drags points. The panel shows the degrees of freedom, offers the constraints the selection can take at its current values, lists the constraints with their dimensions (number or expression), and names and highlights a constraint the solver refuses without changing the drawing. Finish (or Finish and extrude, from the largest closed loop with the loops inside it as holes) is one edit; Escape drops the stroke in progress, then leaves without an edit. Other edits, history and export wait while the mode is open. Sketching is desktop work: at a root width of 800 px or less the entries are disabled with the reason, and read-only hosts have none. Loft… and Sweep… (R1e) in the Sketches section make a loft through the closed regions of chosen sketches in order (ruled or smooth) or sweep one sketch's region along the open chain another draws (Frenet or fixed); each is one edit, and the Inspector shows the sections and edits Ruled and the orientation. Their Result choice (R2a-2b) makes a surface instead: a sheet lofted through the chain each chosen sketch draws (open, or one loop) or the profile sketch's chain swept along the path. It opens with the first named view and offers the others under Views; which view is shown is private view state, not content.
- R2a-2c: a picked edge's Inspector collects it (Collect edge, in pick order, once each) for the Navigator's Boundary… dialog (a boundary surface of 3 or 4 curves or a fill of 3 to 16, sketch curves and collected edges in loop order, `G1` per collected free edge) and for the Loft… dialog's surface result (the collected edges as one section). Each is one edit, and the collected edges are used up when it applies. The Inspector of a fill switches each referenced edge between `G0` and `G1`.
- R2a-3: an active sheet body's Inspector sews it with other sheets (Sew, optionally Close into a solid), trims it with a sketch, an X, Y or Z plane at an offset, or another sheet, keeping a chosen side (Trim), and thickens it (Thicken, towards the front, the back or both). Each is one edit. A `sew-gap` message names the edges left apart. The Inspector of a trim, sew or thicken shows its side, distance or thickness.
- R2a-4a: a picked edge's Inspector checks its continuity (Check continuity): the samples and tolerances, each level's largest deviation with where it is, and whether it is within tolerance at the samples. The report belongs to that pick on that content, and an answer for a pick changed meanwhile is dropped; nothing is edited.
- R2a-4b: the view's Shading offers Zebra (stripes reflected from the display mesh's normals around a world axis, with a stripe count), Mean curvature and Gauss curvature (the kernel's node values for the content on screen, with a legend and a range in powers of two). A picked edge's Inspector shows its curvature comb (Show curvature comb), drawn over the view with a scale; Sketch mode's panel draws every spline's comb. All display only.
- `CadAssembly` (R2b-1, r2b-assembly.md): the Assembly section lists components with their part's state, inserts a saved version from the Host's `CadPartSourceHost` (desktop only; the closure is staged through `CadPartStore` before the edit), edits a fixed component's placement and removes it. Part meshes share the main-thread mesh budget (least recently used versions are released, the rest drawn as boxes) and are drawn instanced (`assembly-view.ts`, on `geometry-view`'s instancing since R2c-3b); a pick returns the component path. Assembly STL and GLB exports are refused (`assembly-export-unsupported`); STEP writes AP242 (R2b-5a, below). R2b-2a (`joints-panel.tsx`, `joint-values.ts`): the Assembly section lists joints with how each solved, edits a joint's values in degrees and mm (an entry is kept across selection like a placement entry and applies as one edit; a value bound to a parameter is changed there), deletes a joint, and offers Solve poses again when stored poses do not match their joints. A joint's child shows which joint places it instead of placement fields. R2b-2b (`part-update-panel.tsx`): a part whose Artifact has a newer saved version is marked (never taken in by itself); Change version… / Update parts… read, check and keep the chosen versions, then preview the edit (`CadController.prepareEdit`: sizes, volumes, components that move, joints that break) and adopt it as one edit, or go on to choosing the first broken joint's connectors again (the R1 reference choice, `rebindEdit`) with it. R2b-2c (`joint-authoring.ts`, `joint-create-panel.tsx`, `joint-drag.ts`): Add joint… (desktop only) turns the viewport's connector tool on: a click picks a face, edge or vertex (vertex within the measure snap, edge within the edge pick distance, hidden ones never) of this document's bodies or a placed part's instance, or an origin is chosen; the child's pick names the direct component it moves. Each choice of connectors, type and flip is prepared as the candidate edit (`prepareEdit`) and shown with its connector frames; Apply joint adopts it as one edit. A selected, solved joint's child (and what hangs from it) can be dragged along the joint's freedoms (Shift: a cylindrical joint's distance, a planar joint's angle): the viewport moves instance matrices only, the Inspector follows, values stop at the limits, and release writes them rounded (0.01 mm, 0.01°) as one edit; Escape cancels. R2b-3a (`bom-panel.tsx`): Bill of materials… shows the flat or hierarchical BOM (`CadAssembly.bom`, also read only and on narrow screens) and exports it as CSV (`onExportCsv`, else a download; the content does not change); a selected component's Count in BOM is one edit (`setComponentBom`); Part properties (in the Elements panel) edits this document's part number and description. R2b-3b (`component-pattern-panel.tsx`): a selected component's Pattern… makes copies of it in a row along X, Y or Z or in a circle about an axis through a point (one edit that selects the pattern); the component list shows each pattern as one collapsed row under its seed (`Bolt ring ×8`), whose Inspector edits its count and spacing or step angle (`dimension-fields.tsx`, shared with the feature Inspector) and removes it. A picked copy selects its pattern, and a selected pattern highlights its seed and copies. A copy can be a joint's parent connector, named by its pattern and number, never its child (`joint-child-copy`). R2b-4a: the binding's port evaluates the parts no joint reads after the result; `CadAssembly.versions` merges them into the result as they arrive (listed as 計算中 until then), the port evaluates them largest on screen first (`CadViewport.screenSize`), the status line shows 「部品 n / N を計算中」 (`progress`) and measurement and PNG export wait for them, while edits and saves do not. R2b-4b: each part job also returns a coarse display mesh (`coarse`), kept apart (`CadAssembly.coarseMeshes`); the viewport draws each instance at a level from its size on screen (standard, coarse, or a solid box; `lodLevel`, levelled again 150 ms after the camera rests, standard while a connector is picked) and writes the counts to the canvas's `data-part-levels`. When the budget runs short, an incoming part keeps its coarse meshes only, or none, and when the editor's share grows the parts smallest on screen lose their standard meshes first, then their coarse ones; such parts are marked 簡略表示 in the component list. R2b-5a: for an assembly, the export dialog offers STEP only, as an AP242 assembly; `CadAssembly.exportStep` waits for every part (`step-parts-pending`) and refuses parts that are not available (`step-part-unavailable`) or stored poses that do not match their joints, plans the products (`assemblyStepPlan`), exports each shaped version once as a BRep with the document's build and has the kernel write the file (`exportAssemblyStep`); the report adds the products and instances. R2b-5b: when the single import's scan finds assembly usages (`NEXT_ASSEMBLY_USAGE_OCCURRENCE` entities, `step-assembly`), Import STEP goes on to `CadAssembly.prepareStepImport`, which takes an empty document only (`step-assembly-not-empty`) and has the kernel read it (`importStepAssembly`); the Import STEP assembly dialog shows the report (format, units, parts, components, depth, split and skipped products, what is not kept). `adoptStepImport` builds the plan (`stepImportPlan` in `cad-core`), stages the source STEP, each shape's BRep and its part document through `CadPartStore`, then, if the document is still empty, places every instance as a fixed component of an `imported` part in one edit; Cancel stores nothing.
- `CadViewport`: Three.js display only (Z-up, mm, orbit, fit, pick, perspective and orthographic named views). A sheet body (R2a-1) is drawn from both sides, its back darker. With Pick faces or Pick edges on, a click picks a face (the triangle's pick id) or the nearest unhidden edge within 8 px and draws it highlighted; the Inspector shows its persistent name, its area or length, and whether a reference could pick it again (unique, by its neighbours' names, or not at all). Picks are evaluation-scoped and dropped when the content changes. The grid follows the model size; orbiting turns half as fast as the OrbitControls default. Meshes from an earlier content are shown dimmed and are not pickable. WebGL loss keeps the lists and Inspector usable. A PNG without background leaves out the background, grid and axes. With the Move tool on, a primary drag on a movable body slides it in its horizontal plane: only the display moves during the gesture, the release makes one `transformBody` edit, and Escape or a cancelled pointer leaves the content unchanged. Pan and zoom stay available; orbit returns when the tool is turned off.

`test-host/` runs the Surface standalone with the development kernel from `CAD_OCCT_DIST` (built outside Git by `npm run cad:kernel`, see `scripts/cad-kernel/README.md`):

```sh
CAD_OCCT_DIST=work/cad-kernel npx vite --config packages/cad/vite.config.ts
CAD_OCCT_DIST=work/cad-kernel E2E_SUITE=cad npx playwright test
```

Query options: `?fixture=plate`, `?fixture=holes` (F12's two holes and the rim fillet), `?fixture=fillet` (F12's top fillet), `?persist=1` (the last applied content is reopened on reload), `?view=side` (with `fixture=plate`: a saved orthographic side view), `?readonly=1`, `?kernel=missing`. See verification.
