# bim (building evaluation and Surface, R2c-3)

Private package of the R2c design (評価と規模): the building domain's kernel evaluation and its Surface. Not a stable public API. Depends on `bim-core`, `geometry-core`, `geometry-kernel-occt`, `geometry-view`, `editor-core` and `editor-ui`, never on a CAD package (ADR 017; ESLint and `geometry-kernel.test.ts` check it).

- `evaluate.ts` (R2c-3a): `BimEvaluator` builds every element's solid from `bimGeometry` and keys it by its recipe (`geometryNodeKey`). Only solids missing from its cache go to the kernel, in batches of at most 64 solids per job; a job that times out or fails validation is split until one solid is left, which fails as `element-timeout` or `graph-invalid`. Elements of one type and size share one solid and its mesh, placed by the element's `placement`. Results per element are its solid's key, placement, display mesh (1 mm, 0.2 rad) and measure, or a code and the failing part of the element (`role`: `solid`, `opening:<id>`, `clip0`, …); other elements still evaluate. A solid over 500,000 triangles fails as `shape-too-large`; a missing BRep asset is not cached, so it can arrive later (and never leaks into another call). Elements whose quantities the analytic derivation cannot give (clipped by a roof) get kernel volumes (`kernelQuantities`, as the IFC export takes them). A call that a later call overtakes returns `superseded`; its solids stay cached. Unused solids beyond 2,000 are dropped, least recently used first.
- `BimKernel` is the kernel side: one batch per call; it answers the evaluation, `timeout` past the job limit, `cancelled` when the call's `signal` aborts (a later evaluation started), or `error` with a code when the kernel cannot run (`kernel-build-unavailable`, `kernel-load-failed`, `kernel-crashed`; not cached). `bimDirectKernel(oc)` runs `evaluateGeometry` in the calling thread without a limit, for tests and tools.
- `port.ts`, `kernel-host.ts`, `worker.ts` (R2c-3c-1): `WorkerBimKernel` runs the kernel in a dedicated Worker (`@editor-platform/bim/worker`) with the Host's verified build: one job at a time; a job past 30 s (start-up included), a cancel not acknowledged within 500 ms, or a crash terminates the Worker, and the next job starts on a fresh one. The Worker cancels cooperatively between graph nodes.
- `controller.ts` (R2c-3c-1): `BimController`, without React. Each edit is one `bimEditorCommand` on the `EditorSession`, applied at once and handed to the Host to save (`onApplied`), then evaluated in the background; a refused edit leaves a `notice` (code and the entities it names) and changes nothing. `sync()` follows the Host's Undo/Redo; `select`, `setLevel`, read-only from `canEdit`.
- `view.ts`, `viewport.ts`, `surface.tsx` (R2c-3c-1): `bimViewSolids` turns the evaluation into `geometry-view` solids (one per shared solid and kind, an instance per element), drawn by `BimViewport` (Three.js, z up, orbit, click to pick). `BimSurface` is the 3D view, the elements of the active level, the selected element's attributes, type, numbers, common properties, property sets and quantities, the level's name and elevation, and the type list with type editing. In the 3D view a click picks; Plan shows the active level from above (orthographic, cut 1,200 mm above the level, pan and zoom only).
- `drafting.ts`, `draft-tools.tsx` (R2c-3c-2): drawing on the plan, React-free where it can be. `bimSnap` takes a grid intersection (kept as a grid reference, so it follows the axes), then an end of a wall on the level, then the 100 mm step. Each drawing is one `insert` operation, so one Undo step: `draftWall` (up to the level above, or 3,000 mm on the top level, with the joins `proposeJoins` gives its ends), `draftRegion` (a slab topped at the level, or a space up to the level above), `draftColumn`, `draftBeam` (topped at the level), and `draftHosted` (a door, window or opening centred on the clicked point of the wall `bimWallAt` finds, kept inside the wall). Walls run on from the end just drawn until Esc or a double-click; slabs and spaces close on the first corner, a double-click or Enter. Drawing is closed, with its reason, when read-only or at 800 px wide and below (390×844); values are still edited there.
- Levels and grid axes (R2c-3c-3a): `draftGrid` draws an axis between two plain points and labels it with the next free letter (along x) or number (along y); `draftLevel` adds a level one storey above the highest. With nothing selected, the inspector edits the level (name, elevation, add, delete) and the grid axes (label and ends; an axis in use is refused and offers the cascade). The view draws the axes dashed, with their labels, on the plan's level or under the lowest level in 3D. With a Host history (`EditorHistoryContext`), Ctrl/Cmd+Z and Shift+Ctrl/Cmd+Z go to it, except in view mode.
- Parts (R2c-3c-3b): `parts.ts` is the Host's `BimPartSource` (`list` the saved CAD versions, `shape` one exact version as a normalized BRep with its bounds and body kinds, `retain` the version with the building). The bim package never reads a CAD document (ADR 017). `addPartType` reads the shape first, retains the version, then inserts a `part` type naming `{ artifactId, versionId, contentHash }` with the bounds as its hint; the Part tool places it on the plan. The controller reads each part type's shape when it opens or adopts a building and passes the bytes to the evaluator under the content hash; a shape it cannot read is shown as the type's bounds with `Unavailable: <code>` and read again next time.
- `drawing.ts` (R2c-5a): `drawBim(doc, view, kernel)` cuts every element the view draws exactly (`section` at the cut plane, filled by its kind and material) in jobs of 64 elements, then projects what lies between the cut and the depth as one `compound` with hidden lines removed, at the fixed drawing tessellation (0.5 mm, 0.1 rad: never the display's LOD), and adds `bimDrawingSymbols`. Plans draw doors and windows by their symbols; sections cut them. An element that cannot be built is listed in `failed` and the rest is drawn. `BimDrawings` caches by content hash, view and part shapes. A drawing never changes the document.
- Views and drawings on screen (R2c-5b): the inspector's Drawings tab lists the views, adds a plan of the active level (`draftPlanView`: cut 1,200 mm, 1:100) and edits a view's name, level, cut height, direction, depth and scale; the Section tool draws a section's line on the plan (`draftSectionView`, looking left). Open drawing shows the view's drawing in place of the 3D view (`BimController.draw`, cached by content; no edit authority needed) and draws again when the building or view changes. `drawing-export.ts` downloads the SVG as drawn or a PNG at 150 dpi on paper (longest side at most 8,192 px).
- IFC in the apps (R2c-6): with an `ifc` prop (the Artifact's ID and the content's time) the toolbar offers Export IFC. `ifc.ts` (`exportBimIfc`, `BimController.exportIfc`) measures what only the kernel knows in its own job, never from the display: the volumes of elements a roof clips, and each part's mesh at a fixed tessellation (0.5 mm, 0.1 rad; a sheet that bounds a volume is written as a closed shell). `bim-ifc` writes the file; a refusal (`part-mixed-kinds`, a part whose shape is not read, an element the kernel cannot build) names the elements in the notice and writes nothing. `@editor-platform/bim/ifc-import` (loaded on demand by the Hosts) reads an `.ifc` file with `readIfc` and shows the loss report (`BimIfcLossDialog`) before the Host makes a new Artifact.
- `BimController` takes any `BimSession` (`content` and `execute`), so a Host can pass its own session (the reference artifact Host passes its `ArtifactEditor`).

`evaluate.test.ts` runs CAD62 and CAD64 against the development build (`CAD_OCCT_DIST`); `ifc.test.ts` covers the IFC export from the app (its kernel case needs `CAD_OCCT_DIST`); `controller.test.ts` and `port.test.ts` cover the controller and the Worker port. `tests/surface.spec.ts` runs in the test host (`E2E_SUITE=bim`, port 4204, `?fixture=starter|box|types|office|gable|hip`, `?readonly=1`, `?kernel=missing`).
