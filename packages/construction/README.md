# construction (4D schedule Surface, CS1b)

Private package of the construction-site plan (CS1b). Not a registered editor or a stable public API. The React Surface over `@editor-platform/construction-core`; the Host supplies the session's edits (`apply`), the scheduled BIM version's content and a BIM kernel.

- `surface.tsx`: `ConstructionSurface`, the task table (start, finish, float, critical) and the task form (name, days, predecessors `a; b(2)`, action, elements from the 3D selection, actual dates and percent), Add/Remove task, CSV import (refused as a whole with its lines) and export, the calendar (start date, working days, holidays), the building version switch, and a day slider with Play over the building's 3D view (`BimViewport`).
- `display.ts`: how each element state is drawn (building orange, removing red, temporary purple; absent left out, or faint with "Show unbuilt"); the slider's days; the form's predecessor text. Rooms and openings are not drawn.
- `site-view.ts` (CS2b): the site's view solids on a day: the ground as one grid over the boundary (moved cells darker), the boundary as a low red band, and each temporary work standing that day as a prism over its outline. Picks carry `site:` IDs.
- `site-panel.tsx` (CS2b): the Site panel: survey points from CSV (`x,y,z`), the boundary points, the earthworks and temporary works with their forms (outline as one `x, y` a line), the day's cut and fill, and the site's problems (temporary works, earthworks and building elements over the boundary).
- `crane-view.ts` (CS3b): cranes as boxes: a tower crane's mast, jib, counter-jib and counterweight, a mobile crane's carrier and boom, the rope and hook at a crane state; `site:crane:` pick IDs.
- `crane-panel.tsx` (CS3b): the Cranes panel: the crane table and form (load chart one `radius, kg` a line), adding or removing a task's lifts from a pick point, the day's lifts with their masses, lift playback (2.5 s a lift, the load moving with the hook) and the crane problems, checked after the frame is drawn against the evaluated building's triangles.
- `analysis-view.ts` (CS4b): the frame analysis against the completed building: its comparison rows (worst ratio first), the look of each member by its ratio (≤ 1.1× green, > 1.1× orange, > 1.5× red), what a frame sends to the Host, and the `FrameSolver` the Host supplies.
- `traffic-view.ts` (CS5b): people and trucks in the view: each danger as a fence round its outline (tall while in force at the time shown), each route as a strip on the ground, and the trips at a time of day: walkers (the blocky figure's own walk, turned to their way) and trucks.
- `traffic-panel.tsx` (CS5b): the People and trucks panel: working hours, routes and danger zones with their forms, a time of day with Play, the day's approaches to danger (a row moves the clock there) and each gate's count.
- Exports (CS6b, in `surface.tsx`): Export IFC writes the building, the schedule and the temporary works as one IFC4 file; Export checks writes the day's checks (schedule, site, cranes, frame, traffic) as one CSV. Neither writes while a form holds unsaved input.
- Video and still (CS6c, `schedule-video.ts`, `surface.tsx`): Record video plays From to To at 30 fps, each day held for 30 ÷ Days a second frames, and writes an H.264 MP4 (`encodeMp4` from `@editor-platform/character/video`) through `BimViewport.beginCapture`; the plan is held still (edits, Undo, exports refused) until it ends or is cancelled. Render image opens the path tracer on the day's view.
- `analysis-panel.tsx` (CS4b): the Frame analysis panel: site loads on the selected slabs and their form, Analyse the day (the day's frame and the completed one solved on the Host at once), the members against the completed building, the frame's notes, and each crane's foundation reaction on the day and over the plan.
