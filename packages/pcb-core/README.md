# pcb-core (PB1–PB7a, pure circuit board model)

Private pure TypeScript model for the printed circuit board (PCB) domain of the PCB plan and ADR 021. No DOM, React, Worker or geometry kernel (the 3D model is a `geometry-core` graph a kernel runs), and no import of another domain's package.

- `model.ts`: `schemaVersion: 1` types, the only schema (`PcbDocument`: board, holes, footprints, placed parts, net classes and nets), millimetres, plan coordinates seen from the top with y up. The board has an outline and cut-outs (loops of `[x, y]` or `[x, y, bulge]` vertices, arcs by bulge as in `geometry-core`), a finished thickness, 2/4/6/8 copper layers, mask and silkscreen colours and a finish. `pcbLayerNames` lists the layers in stack order (KiCad-style names). `pcbLimits` and `pcbTolerance` hold the initial safety limits.
- `validatePcb(unknown)`: strict plain-JSON validation with path diagnostics: exact keys, one ID namespace, ranges and enums, simple loops (exact along arcs), cut-outs inside the outline and apart, holes wholly in the board's material, off cut-outs, apart, and keep-outs larger than their drill. Any `schemaVersion` other than 1 is `unsupported-schema`; there is no migration.
- Footprints (PB2): pads (`smd`, `thru`, `npth`; circle, rect, oval or rounded rectangle, turned by multiples of 90°), silkscreen lines and circles, a courtyard loop, a body height and the generator that made them. Parts (`PcbComponent`) place a footprint by reference designator, position, rotation and side; the bottom side mirrors x.
- `generators.ts`: twelve parametric footprint generators (pin header, chip 0402–1206, SOIC, QFN, BGA, FPC, JST SH, USB-C, micro HDMI, stacked USB-A, RJ45, plain body), written from the general form of each package, not copied from any maker's drawing. Bad numbers throw `PcbGeneratorError` (`generator-param:<name>`).
- `placement.ts`: exact pad outlines, the part → board transform, placed pads with their copper layers, and `pcbPlacementProblems` (courtyard overlaps on one side, parts leaving the board, parts over a hole), which are shown, never refused.
- `applyPcbOperation`: pure, atomic operations (`insert`, `update`, `updateMany`, `delete`, `setBoard`); a footprint in use cannot be deleted without its parts (`in-use`), each result validated as a whole. The caller's trusted layer assigns every new ID.
- `pcbEditorCommand`: one `EditorSession` transaction per operation. It re-checks the expected base `contentHash` (`pcb-base-mismatch`) and edit authority (`permission-denied`), and pins `reads.content = ['*']`.
- `applyPcbOperations`: several operations applied in turn (each with its own checks), the result validated once as a whole. `pcbBatchCommand`: that as one transaction (one Undo), with the same base and authority checks; used to send a schematic to the board (SL3).
- `geometry.ts`: loop helpers (area, bounds, simplicity, containment, distance, `roundedRectLoop`).
- `fixtures.ts`: `pcbStarter` and F75 `f75Pi5Board` (Raspberry Pi 5 form factor from the public mechanical drawing: 85 × 56 mm, R3, four Ø2.7 mm mounting holes).

- `nets.ts` (PB3): nets join pads by `[partId, padNumber]` (a pad in one net at most) under a net class (track width, clearance, via size); `pcbRatsnest` joins each net's pins by a minimum spanning tree; `pcbImportNetList` reads `{ "nets": [{ "name", "class"?, "pins": ["J1.6"] }] }` by reference designators. Deleting a part takes its pins out of their nets; a class in use is kept (`in-use`).
- `connectivity.ts` (PB4a): tracks (`net`, `layer`, `widthMm`, `from`, `to`) and vias (`net`, `at`, `diameterMm`, `drillMm`) join pads into copper groups per layer (pads as polygons, tracks as capsules, vias on every copper layer); `pcbRatsnest` leaves out pins already joined by copper. A net that still carries tracks or vias is kept (`in-use`).
- `scanbeam.ts` and `zones.ts` (PB4b): zones (`net`, `layer`, `outline`, `priority`, `clearanceMm`, `padConnection`, `thermalGapMm`, `spokeWidthMm`) are filled by `pcbZoneFills`, the one fill the screen, DRC and Gerber share: clear of the board edge and cut-outs (`board.edgeClearanceMm`), mounting and unplated holes (`board.holeClearanceMm` or the keep-out), other nets' copper (the larger of the zone's and the net class clearance) and higher-priority zones of other nets; same-net pads join by spokes or solid; islands without the net's copper are dropped. The fill is trapezoids from a scanbeam polygon Boolean; curves become chords drawn outside the true shape (0.005 mm). `pcbRatsnest(doc, fills?)` counts zone connections.
- `drc.ts` (PB5): `pcbDrc(doc, fills?)` checks copper of other nets (shorts and clearances, zone fills included), edge and cut-out clearance, mounting/unplated hole clearance (keep-outs included), track width, drill size, annular ring, unconnected pins, silkscreen over pads and placement problems; each finding is `{ rule, severity, items, at, layer?, distanceMm?, requiredMm? }`. Neighbours come from `@editor-platform/spatial-index`. The board holds the rules (`edgeClearanceMm`, `holeClearanceMm`, `minTrackWidthMm`, `minDrillMm`, `minAnnularRingMm`); clearances come from net classes and zones. Pads are measured as polygons that hold their true arcs (`padPolygon`), the same ones the fills avoid.
- `gerber.ts` (PB6): `pcbFabricationFiles(doc, base?, fills?)` writes Gerber X2 per layer (copper, mask as negative, paste, silkscreen, profile; format 4.6 mm; `.FileFunction`, `.FilePolarity`, `.AperFunction`, `.TO.N/P/C`; arcs as G75 arcs; zone copper as one region per fill trapezoid from the shared `pcbZoneFills`), Excellon drill files (plated, unplated; mm), a BOM and a placement CSV. `pcbFabricationZip` zips them with a fixed time, so the same board gives the same bytes. `gerber-read.ts` is a test-only reader of exactly what is written (not exported from the package index).
- `model3d.ts` (PB7a): `pcbModel3d(doc)` gives the board and its parts as a `geometry-core` op graph and a list of coloured pieces (`{ node, name, component?, material, color }`). The board is its outline swept by its thickness (bottom face at z = 0) with its cut-outs and drilled holes (vias dropped first past the kernel's 1,000-hole limit, counted in `omittedHoles`). Each part is a simplified shape from its generator's numbers (body, pins, leads, metal shell with its port cut out), placed by a rigid transform: bottom parts hang under the board, mirrored by a half turn about y.
