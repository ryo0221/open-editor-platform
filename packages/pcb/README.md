# pcb (PB1–PB7c, circuit board Surface)

Private React Surface for the circuit board domain (PCB plan, ADR 021). It edits a `pcb-core` document through `PcbController`, a React-free controller whose every edit is one `EditorSession` command; the Host saves and owns Undo/Redo.

- `PcbSurface`: an SVG plan of the board (mm, y up) with wheel zoom about the cursor, drag or Shift/middle/right-drag to pan, a grid (0.05–10 mm, 2.54 mm included) and snapping; tools to select and drag mounting holes, add holes by clicking and draw an outline by clicking corners (Enter or the first corner closes, Esc cancels); a left panel with holes and layers; a Place part tool (pick a generator and its numbers in the inspector, click to place; equal numbers share one footprint); parts drawn with pads, drills, silkscreen, courtyard and reference (bottom-side parts see-through), selected by click (Ctrl/Cmd-click adds), dragged as one edit, turned with R (Shift+R back), flipped with F, deleted with Delete, locked parts left alone; parts, nets and placement problems listed on the left; a ratsnest with the selected net lit; a Route track tool (active copper layer, 45° corners, V drops a via and switches between the outer layers, clicking copper of the same net finishes, Enter ends, Esc drops the run; widths and via sizes come from the net class) with tracks and vias drawn per layer colour, picked and edited in a Track / Via inspector; a Draw zone tool (net picked in the inspector, corners on the active layer) with zone fills drawn from one `pcbZoneFills` result shared with the ratsnest, a Zone inspector (net, layer, priority, clearance, thermal or solid pads, gap, spoke width, filled area) board design rules, and a live DRC list (errors and warnings; a click selects what is involved and centres the view) with markers on the plan; a Fabrication section (file name, one zip of Gerber, drill, BOM and placement files, a warning while DRC errors remain); a pin table per part (pad → net), a net inspector (name, class, pins as `REF.PAD`), net classes and a JSON net list import in the board inspector; an inspector for the board (size, area, a rounded rectangle from the origin, thickness, copper layers, mask, silkscreen, finish) the selected hole (name, position, drill, keep-out, plated, delete) or the selected part (reference, value, position, rotation, side, locked). Refusals show their code.
- 3D view (PB7b): the toolbar's 3D view button swaps the plan for `PcbView3d`. The board and parts come from `pcbModel3d` (`pcb-core`), built by the shared geometry kernel in a dedicated Worker (`kernel.ts`: `WorkerPcbKernel`, one job at a time, 30 s, a crash or a stopped job starts a fresh Worker; `worker.ts` is the `./worker` entry); a host without a kernel build gets a message instead. `viewport3d.ts` draws the pieces in their materials' colours with orbit, pan and zoom (Fit, Top, Bottom). `layer-art.ts` paints each face (mask, copper under it, pads where the mask opens in the finish's colour, silkscreen, clear holes) on a sheet laid just off the board (`scene3d.ts`). Export GLB writes every piece and both face pictures (`glb.ts`, metres and Y up, the pictures as PNG with a mask alpha).
- `view.ts`: pure view math (fit, zoom, screen ↔ board, grid snapping, SVG paths exact along arcs).
- Test host: `npx vite --config packages/pcb/vite.config.ts` (port 4207), `?fixture=starter|f75`, `?readonly=1`. Browser tests: `E2E_SUITE=pcb npx playwright test`.

- STEP (PB7c): Export STEP (AP214) and (AP242) run the same model once more with a `GeometryStepRequest` (`pcbStepRequest`): one product per piece, named and coloured as in the view, under a product named for the file.

- From schematic (SL3b): with a `PcbSchematicSource` from the Host, the board inspector lists the Host's saved schematic versions; the chosen version's diff against the board (`schematicBoardDiff` from `schematic-core`) is a checklist, and Apply runs the kept changes as one edit (`PcbController.applyBatch`, one Undo). Pads two schematic nets share are shown and block Apply.
