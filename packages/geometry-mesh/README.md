# geometry-mesh (domain-independent polygon meshes, ADR 019)

Private package of the [Character design](../../docs/character/README.md) and ADR 019. Not a registered editor or a stable public API. Depends on `@noble/hashes` only, never on a CAD, BIM or character package (the ADR 017 rule for `geometry-*`).

- `epm.ts`: `EpmMesh` (polygon faces of any size, per-corner UVs, edge creases, variable-count skin weights, per-vertex morph deltas; millimetres) and its immutable byte form EPM 1. `encodeEpm` gives the same bytes for the same mesh; `decodeEpm(bytes, limits)` checks the header, the section table (known tags, 4-byte aligned, no overlap, required sections), the JSON meta and every array (lengths, finite values, face sizes 3–64 without repeated vertices, index ranges, crease pairs, weights per vertex that sum to 1 with distinct bones, unique bone and morph IDs) against `epmLimits`, and refuses anything else with `MeshAssetError` (`mesh-asset-invalid`). Nothing is repaired. `epmSha256` names the bytes as content does (`sha256:<hex>`).
- `display.ts`: `meshDisplay(mesh)` splits each face corner into a display vertex, splits polygons into triangles by ear clipping in their Newell plane (concave faces keep their outline and winding; `triangulateFace`), grouped by material slot, gives smooth normals (Newell, area-weighted), four skin influences per vertex (`fourInfluences`, the largest kept and renormalised) and morph deltas per display vertex. `meshBounds`.
- `primitives.ts`: `boxMesh(size, center)`, a box of six quads.
- `topology.ts` (C3): `meshTopology(mesh)`, the half-edge view (half-edge = face corner, `twin`, outgoing half-edges per vertex). It refuses, with `MeshEditError('non-manifold')`, an edge used twice in one direction (more than two faces, or flipped neighbours) and a vertex whose faces form more than one fan. Borders are allowed. `faceNormal`, `faceVertices`, `meshEdges`, `edgeKey`.
- `edit.ts` (C3a): pure edits from one `EpmMesh` to a new one. `editInput(mesh)` checks the mesh and builds its topology; parts are edited up to `meshEditLimits.vertices` (200,000). Edits: `transformVertices` / `translateVertices`, `extrudeFaces` (one region along its mean normal), `insetFaces` (each face on its own), `deleteFaces`, `mergeVertices`, `setEdgeCreases`. New vertices carry blended skin weights (largest 8 kept, renormalised), morph deltas and per-corner UVs. The result drops unused vertices, keeps creases on edges that still exist, and is checked again (EPM checks and manifold). Every refusal is a `MeshEditError` (`non-manifold`, `selection`, `degenerate`, `limit`), and the input is never changed.
- `edit-loops.ts` (C3b): `edgeLoop` (straight across 4-edge vertices, stops at others and borders), `edgeRing` (across quads), `loopCut` (1–32 evenly spaced loops; faces at the ends of an open ring gain the vertices), `bevelEdges` (one segment, offset `width` along the neighbouring edges, a polygon closes each corner; border edges and border vertices are refused), `knifeFace` (one straight cut across a face between corners or edge points; the neighbour gains the edge point), and symmetry: `mirrorVertices` (pairs across a plane within a tolerance), `mirroredVertexSelection`, `mirroredFaceSelection`, `translateSymmetric` (on-plane vertices stay on the plane). `extrudeFaces` moves each connected region along its own mean normal, so a mirrored pair of faces extrudes apart.
- `subdivide.ts` (C3c): `subdivideMesh(mesh, levels)`, Catmull-Clark with creases (0–1 blends each rule toward the sharp one; borders are sharp; a border corner of one face stays put; child edges keep the parent crease). UVs are subdivided linearly per face, skin weights and morph deltas follow the position stencils, materials stay per face. `subdivisionLimits` (4 levels, 1,000,000 faces) is checked with `subdividedFaceCount` before any work. It is a display mesh: the part's asset stays the cage.
