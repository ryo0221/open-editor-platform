# Commercial / OEM agreement — contract and evidence guide

**Applies only when the customer chooses alternative commercial rights instead of complying with AGPLv3.** This document is NOT a price quote, legal advice or a license grant.

## AGPL is already a valid commercial route
GNU AGPLv3 permits paid distribution and revenue-generating network software when all terms applicable to the work and its deployment are satisfied. Neither an owner's notice nor a checkbox may add extra mandatory restrictions to the AGPL grant already received. Do **not** treat all commercial operators as prospective paying customers.

## Alternative-license agreement
For a company seeking proprietary covered-combination distribution or network deployment inconsistent with AGPL, obtain a **separate written agreement** from all necessary rights holders. Terms should specify the legal parties, licensed version and source, products, territories, right to modify and redistribute, patents, downstream rights, term, fees if any, notice/compliance, support exclusion, liability and dispute resolution.

Commercial licensor provides code and defined rights, not automatically product integration, updates, testing, security support, warranties or SLAs. Those are the licensee's responsibilities unless explicitly agreed otherwise.

## Cross-border contract evidence
If such an alternative deal is executed, retain a secure record of: offered terms/version/hash, actual contract, signatory's identity and organizational authority, conspicuous notice of **commercial contract** obligations and exclusions, timestamp, chosen language, explanations requested/provided and both signatures.

The United States has consent/notice questions for clickwrap/browsewrap. The EU has non-waivable software and consumer rights. Mainland China places requirements on conspicuous standard terms and explanations; mere checkboxes may be inadequate evidence.

These safeguards concern **optional commercial contracting**. The GNU AGPL rights available without such a contract are not conditioned on signing it.
