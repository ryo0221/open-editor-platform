Shared infrastructure is limited to controls, panel/focus behavior, transaction lifecycle, stable addresses, geometry calculations, coordinate conversion and selection rendering. Each surface owns its model and interaction policy. Camera state, panel state and unsaved view preferences stay outside artifact content/hash/Undo. Saving a named Data view explicitly persists its definition without copying records or derived chart values.

## Whiteboard Section rules

- No nested Sections and no simultaneous membership in several Sections. Section IDs live separately from node/group IDs. A root node or Group has one optional `sectionId`; a grouped child inherits the root's membership.
- A committed root move chooses the last/topmost Section containing the root's center. Outside all Sections clears membership. Rendering and Section resizing do not recompute membership. Editing a child inside a Group does not reassign the whole Group; ungroup first to move that child independently between Sections.
- Move translates members once, including nested Groups. Resize preserves all member geometry and membership. Section rotation is unsupported. Section dissolve removes only the region and membership; delete-with-contents is a separate command.
- Sections do not clip content. Section exports include the region plus all its members, their overflow, and internal/free connectors. Connections to nonmembers are omitted from that scoped export; whole-board export retains them.
- Legacy Whiteboard frames become Sections in the editable draft. Legacy Group nodes remain Groups. IDs and world geometry are retained, nested legacy frames flatten to separate Sections, and each node inherits the nearest legacy frame membership. Immutable saved versions remain unchanged.
- Camera zoom is bounded for usable rendering; world position is not clamped to page edges. Ordinary editing supports at least ±50,000 coordinates. Viewport rectangles are derived from the camera and never persisted as paper size.
