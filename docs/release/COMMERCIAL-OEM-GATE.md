# Commercial/OEM licensing — later launch gate

**Status:** postponed until after the AGPLv3 Preview launch. No published tariff, no automatic royalty and no commitment to supply proprietary commercial licenses merely because a customer requests them.

AGPL-compliant commercial products and compliant SaaS can continue to use the project without paying an OEM fee. Alternative commercial licensing is only needed when a customer needs **additional permissions beyond AGPL**.

## Before the first paid commercial/OEM agreement

- [ ] Verify real contracting legal entity and authority to sign and sublicense every relevant copyrighted file, including accepted future external contributions.
- [ ] Finalize contributor rights/CLA intake to support proprietary licensing; do not treat OSS PR acceptance as automatic commercial permission.
- [ ] Identify owned/controlled patent claims and licenses; distinguish AGPLv3 §11 grants and third-party patent rights from rights a licensor can grant commercially.
- [ ] Agree scope, actual supplied release/commit, covered products, closed-source permissions, distribution/hosting, term, renewal, fees, dependencies, downstream rights, warranty/indemnity and termination.
- [ ] State clearly that the licensor does not supply integration, hosting, updates, security patches, technical support or SLA except by a separately signed agreement. The licensee maintains, tests, secures and supports its own product.
- [ ] Include only verified third-party rights, licenses and notices; proprietary OEM terms cannot override third-party licenses.
- [ ] Prepare a standard written B2B contract, signatory verification, jurisdictional notices and acceptance records. Seek qualified legal review before the first substantive cross-border deal or when unresolved rights issues appear.
- [ ] Decide whether to implement the **independently authored Editor exception** only after actual versioned Extension Contract implementation and owner/legal rights analysis. A draft is not effective.

## Operational model

Prefer a simple product-specific license and low-support overhead. Pricing (flat annual fee, etc.) is a future commercial decision. No usage tracking, maintenance/SLA staffing, marketplace or global legal rollout is required for the AGPL OSS Preview.

**Never make paid use alone a license violation.** AGPLv3 is a valid free alternative when satisfied. Historical MIT permissions remain valid for prior copies received under MIT.
